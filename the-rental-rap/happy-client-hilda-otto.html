The Rental Wrap

Case Study

Customer Profile - Hilda and Otto*

16 December 2025

The challenging market conditions are making it harder than ever, particularly if your are relocating from Europe. This was the case with Hilda and Otto. Hilda was enrolled to start at university, so their time frame was set. They also had young children, so wanted to be in a safe, family-friendly area with plenty of amenities.

In Hilda and Otto’s own words:

From the very first contact to finally receiving the approval for our new home, our experience with Wendy and the entire Your Home Hunter team has been nothing short of exceptional. We had tried applying for properties from Germany on our own, but the process was incredibly confusing and difficult to navigate. We often didn’t know which documents were required, how the application process worked, or how to present ourselves properly. This is where YHH became an absolute lifesaver. They took over the entire process for us – clearly, professionally, and with so much heart. It was truly worth its weight in gold.

Wendy and her team handled everything: communicating with agents, preparing all the documents, guiding us step by step, and organising a viewing while we were still overseas. At every moment, we felt supported, informed, and genuinely cared for.

We honestly couldn’t have wished for better support. It was always clear that Wendy and her team went above and beyond to give us the best possible start in our new home – and they absolutely succeeded.

Moving to a new country is a huge step, but having people like Wendy by your side makes all the difference. We are incredibly grateful and more than satisfied. A wonderful team that truly changes lives.

Hilda & Otto

* Names have been changed to protect the privacy of the individuals involved.
