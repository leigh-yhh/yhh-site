Exciting Opportunity for Seniors

26 November 2023

Are you or someone you know retired, but still want to work a few hours each month? Do you like meeting new people and creating new connections? Are you passionate about property? If so, this is the opportunity for you…

To help meet increasing demand for our service, we’re looking to engage a committed team of local seniors. This team will play a critical role in helping tenants navigate the highly competitive rental market by inspecting properties of interest and confirming they fulfil their needs and preferences. Depending on the client's circumstances, this may be done with them in person or on their behalf via video call.

As our service is client-driven, Inspectors will be engaged on an as-needed basis. To provide further flexibility, we’re also planning to have a handful of team members in each area, allowing Inspectors to choose the opportunities that best fit their schedule.

For more information on becoming a Your Home Hunter Inspector, or to register your interest, check out our Careers page.
