About

Who we are

Your Home Hunter was founded on a simple belief: everyone deserves to live somewhere they love. We have been tenants, landlords, and property managers, so we know how hard it can be to find and secure the right rental.

Since 2018 we have helped hundreds of renters across Melbourne, Brisbane, Canberra, and Adelaide. That includes executives, officials, athletes, young professionals, growing families, and people relocating from interstate or overseas.

Wendy, a licensed real estate agent, started the business. Brittany now leads the team day to day, with Wendy still involved in an advisory role.
