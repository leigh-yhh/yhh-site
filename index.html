Locations

Melbourne, Brisbane, Canberra and Adelaide

We attend inspections in these four cities. If you are moving somewhere else, we can still help with applications and search strategy.

Victoria

Melbourne

Vacancy is about 1.8%, close to the old balanced mark, but the city does not move as one market. Inner suburbs and well-priced homes still lease quickly, while some other pockets have eased. The rush is November to February, when students and people starting new jobs arrive.

Popular suburbs: South Yarra, Richmond, St Kilda, Prahran, Fitzroy, Carlton, Brunswick, Northcote, Fairfield, Hawthorn, Brighton, CBD

Queensland

Brisbane

Vacancy has fallen to about 0.9%. For a long time it sat above 2%, before a steady move north from the southern states. Work towards the 2032 Olympics is expected to keep that pressure on, with the busiest inspections between November and January.

Popular suburbs: New Farm, Teneriffe, Fortitude Valley, Paddington, West End, Kangaroo Point, Bulimba, Ascot, Hamilton, South Brisbane

ACT

Canberra

The steadiest of the four, with vacancy around 2.1%. Parliament, the public service, and the universities give the year a clearer rhythm: strongest in January and February, quietest in June, then busier again in spring.

Popular suburbs: Kingston, Barton, Braddon, New Acton, Yarralumla, Forrest, Campbell, Griffith, Deakin, Belconnen

South Australia

Adelaide

The tightest of the four, with vacancy around 0.6%. It was one of the more affordable capitals for a long time. Homes near the CBD, the beach, and the hills now go quickly, and the tunnel and submarine projects are bringing more workers in.

Popular suburbs: North Adelaide, Norwood, Unley, Prospect, Glenelg, Bowden, Hyde Park, Parkside, Burnside, Mile End

FAQ

Common questions

What does a renters advocate do?

A renters advocate works only for the tenant - not landlords or agencies. We take your brief, search on- and off-market properties, inspect homes on your behalf (including live video), help with applications on platforms like 2Apply and Snug, review the lease, and help you get the keys.

Can you inspect properties if I live interstate or overseas?

Yes. Many of our clients are moving from interstate or overseas. Inspectors attend viewings in Melbourne, Brisbane, Canberra, and Adelaide, and can join you live on Zoom, WhatsApp, or FaceTime, plus send a condition report, so you do not have to sign a lease sight unseen.

Do you have access to off-market rental properties?

Yes. Since 2018 we have built relationships with property managers, agencies, and private landlords. That often means we hear about homes before they appear on realestate.com.au or Domain.

How do you help our application get approved when vacancy is under 1%?

When vacancy is tight, incomplete or unusual applications are easy to overlook. We check your paperwork, help you present your situation clearly, brief your referees, speak with the leasing agent, and lodge the application promptly after a viewing.

Which cities across Australia does Your Home Hunter service?

We inspect properties in Melbourne, Brisbane, Canberra, and Adelaide. For other cities, including Sydney, Perth, Newcastle, the Central Coast, and the Gold Coast, we can still help with search strategy, application reviews, and tenancy advice.

How does pricing work and what services do you offer?

Pricing is listed above. Property Review & Inspection starts at a fixed fee. Full Service is quoted from your brief. Tenancy Consultations are $165 including GST for a 60 minute discussion. There is also a free DIY search guide if you want to go it alone.
