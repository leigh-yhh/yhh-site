The Fine Print

This Service Agreement is made on the date stated above between YHH Pty Ltd (“YHH”, “Your Home Hunter”) and the Client named above. The Client wishes to engage YHH to undertake a search on the terms and conditions set out here.

Services

YHH will undertake the following tasks (unless agreed otherwise) on behalf of the Client:

  1. Taking instructions from the Client, including preferred location(s), size, amenities, weekly rental, and other requirements
  2. Searching for available properties that meet as many of the Client’s criteria as possible
  3. Advising the Client of a short list and which property(s) are the best match
  4. Inspecting the Client’s preferred properties (with or without the Client present, if required)
  5. Completing and submitting, or assisting the Client to complete and submit, rental application(s)
  6. Advocating for the Client to the property manager / landlord in support of the application
  7. Completing sundry and associated tasks required to deliver the service

YHH bases the search on the Client’s stated requirements in the New Home Wish List. If those requirements materially change (for example preferred location or budget), additional fees may apply. YHH will discuss this with the Client first.

Client acknowledgements

The Client agrees and acknowledges that:

  1. While YHH provides recommendations and advice, the Client is solely responsible for carrying out any steps arising from that advice
  2. YHH is neither a lawyer nor an accountant; recommendations are not legal or financial advice
  3. Nothing in this Agreement results in YHH being considered a co-applicant on any rental agreement

Guarantee

YHH cannot guarantee results, as outcomes depend on the match between the Client’s requirements and available properties. YHH will use best endeavours to help the Client achieve a successful outcome.

Commencement

This Agreement commences on the date above and continues until the earliest of: (a) 90 days (unless agreed otherwise); (b) a rental application is accepted; or (c) termination in accordance with this Agreement.

Termination

Either party may terminate for any reason by providing the other party with 24 hours’ written notice. If terminated after all search tasks have been provided, the Client must pay the full Fee. If terminated after part of the search tasks, the Client must pay for consulting hours and out-of-pocket expenses incurred before termination.

Standard of care

In carrying out the search, YHH will: (a) exercise the skill, diligence and prudence reasonably expected from a competent professional in the industry; (b) comply with applicable laws; and (c) maintain any necessary licences and authorisations.

Fees

The Client agrees to pay the Fee quoted in the emailed offer for YHH providing the search. Fees are exclusive of GST unless stated otherwise and are fixed unless varied in writing. Tasks undertaken by the Client are not deducted from the Fee unless agreed in writing. Invoices are payable on the due date. Engagement Fee is payable now to commence the search; the remaining service fee is invoiced after the Client signs a lease (or as otherwise quoted).

Client to provide information

The Client will: (a) provide relevant information including criminal and rental history requested by YHH; and (b) promptly respond to reasonable requests for further information or instruction.

Confidentiality

YHH will not, during or after this Agreement, disclose confidential Client information except as required to provide the Services or as required by law.

Liability

(a) Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, special or consequential loss. (b) Subject to that, a party’s liability arising in connection with this Agreement is limited to the aggregate value of Fees paid.

Entire agreement & governing law

To the extent permitted by law, this Agreement embodies the entire understanding of the parties. It is governed by the laws of Victoria, Australia.

Privacy

By signing, you consent to YHH collecting, holding, using and disclosing your personal information to deliver the Services, including lodging applications and speaking with agents.
