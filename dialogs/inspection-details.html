Your Unbiased Eyes & Ears On The Ground

Real estate photos, wide-angle lenses, and curated staging frequently disguise poor maintenance, mould, street noise, and layout defects. If you cannot attend inspections in person, our tenant advocates attend on your behalf and deliver an unvarnished assessment of the property.

Comprehensive Inspection Inclusions

Pre-Inspection Due Diligence & Listing Audit

Prior to arriving on site, we review the listing history, council zoning, public transport proximity, flight paths, and nearby active construction developments that could impact your quiet enjoyment.

Physical Attendance & Live Walkthrough

We physically attend the scheduled open-for-inspection or private viewing. Join us in real time via live video stream (Zoom, WhatsApp, or FaceTime), or receive a high-definition 4K recorded video tour with live commentary.

Defect, Condition & Livability Check

We test water pressure and drainage, inspect under-sink cabinetry and ceilings for damp or odour, test window seals and locks, assess storage capacity, examine natural light angles, and inspect heating and cooling systems.

Streetscape, Noise & Parking Assessment

We evaluate street safety, traffic noise levels, neighbourhood upkeep, permit parking availability, and proximity to neighbouring buildings or commercial venues.

Direct Property Manager Engagement

We speak directly with the leasing agent on site to uncover applicant volume, landlord expectations, preferred move-in dates, and maintenance commitments before you submit.
