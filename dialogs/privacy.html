Introduction and Purpose

YHH Pty Ltd trading as Your Home Hunter (“we”, “us”, or “our”) provides renters’ advocacy, property search, video walkthrough inspection, and tenancy consulting services across Australia.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal and sensitive information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we handle and protect it, who we disclose it to, and your rights regarding your personal records.

Personal Information We Collect

Because we advocate for tenants and submit rental applications on their behalf, we collect personal and sensitive information directly from clients, including:

  • Identity Documentation: Full legal names, dates of birth, residential addresses, contact telephone numbers, email addresses, emergency contacts, and copies of government-issued identity documents (passports, driver licences, citizenship records).
  • Financial & Employment Records: Payslips, employment agreements, accountant verification letters, proof of funds, and bank statements showing living allowances.
  • Tenancy Background: Rental ledgers, past landlord or agent reference contacts, personal and professional character references, pet profiles, and detailed housing criteria.
  • Payment Details: Direct debit and credit card details collected via our PCI-compliant payment gateway (Square and Pinch Payments) and banking channels (National Australia Bank). We do not store full credit card numbers on our internal servers.
  • Website & Technical Data: IP addresses, browser types, device identifiers, and site usage data collected through our website host and Google Analytics (when enabled).

How We Collect Information

We collect personal information primarily directly from you when you:

  • Complete our online inquiry, contact, search brief, or Full Service Package forms on this website (or via DocuSign / Stripe when those links are provided).
  • Sign our Client Service Agreement.
  • Transmit identity, financial, and employment records via email, file sharing, or form upload.
  • Communicate with our advocates and inspectors by email, phone, or instant messaging.

Why We Collect and How We Use Your Information

We collect, hold, and use your information exclusively to:

  • Assess your property search brief, criteria, and budget.
  • Inspect properties and deliver detailed video walkthrough reports.
  • Prepare, complete, and submit competitive residential tenancy applications on your behalf.
  • Advocate with real estate agents, property managers, and private landlords.
  • Review lease documents and tenancy agreements.
  • Invoice and process service fees.
  • Comply with statutory tax, corporate, and accounting record-keeping obligations under Australian law.

Disclosures to Third Parties & Rental Portals

To deliver our advocacy services, we disclose your documentation to third parties strictly on your instructions:

  • Real Estate Agents & Landlords: We provide tenancy applications, identity documents, financial verifications, and references directly to prospective agents and property owners.
  • Online Tenancy Application Portals: Many Australian leasing agencies mandate application lodgement through third-party platforms, including 2Apply, Snug, Ignite (realestate.com.au), and TenantApp. When we submit an application for you, your data is processed through those platforms subject to their independent privacy policies and terms of service. Note: We cannot control or alter data retention and destruction schedules operated independently by these external portals.
  • Operational Cloud Suppliers: We use secure software platforms to operate our business, including Google Workspace, Zoho CRM / Forms, Xero, and Pinch Payments.
  • Legal Requirements: Where required or authorised by Australian law, court order, or regulatory body.

Overseas Disclosures & International Clients

Our business operates within Australia. However:

  • Cloud Infrastructure: Cloud platforms we use (such as Google Workspace and Zoho) may store or replicate data across secure data centres located overseas, including in the United States.
  • Inbound Relocations: We routinely represent expatriates and international clients relocating to Australia. By engaging our services and submitting your application documents from outside Australia, you consent to the cross-border transfer, storage, and processing of your personal data within Australia to enable the sourcing and securing of an Australian rental property.

Data Retention, Storage, and Secure Destruction

  • Application & Verification Documents: Highly sensitive identification documents (passports, driver licences), bank statements, and payslips held directly on our internal storage are permanently deleted or de-identified within thirty (30) days after placement, completion, or formal termination of your service agreement.
  • Statutory Records: Executed agreements, tax invoices, and accounting records are maintained for five (5) to seven (7) years as required under the Corporations Act 2001 (Cth) and Australian taxation legislation.
  • Security Controls: We implement TLS/HTTPS encryption on web forms, role-based access control, and multi-factor authentication (MFA) across our digital workspaces to protect data from loss, misuse, or unauthorised access.

Website Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses cookies and tracking technologies:
