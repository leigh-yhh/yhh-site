Targeted Strategic Advice For Renters

Whether you keep getting rejected in Australia's competitive rental markets, need an expert eye to audit your application before submitting, or are navigating a stressful tenancy dispute, our 1-on-1 consultation connects you directly with a tenant advocate.

What We Cover in Your Consultation

Application Portfolio & Profile Audit

We review your complete application dossier across 2Apply, Snug, Ignite, or paper forms. We pinpoint hidden red flags in your employment documentation, proof of funds, rental ledgers, and referee presentation that cause leasing agents to pass over your profile.

Rejection Diagnosis & Market Repositioning

If you've been applying without success, we diagnose the bottlenecks such as rent-to-income affordability calculations, personal identification gaps, or submission delays to build an immediate, winning application strategy.

Expat, Interstate & Non-Standard Income Strategy

Moving from interstate or overseas without Australian rental history? Self-employed, contracting, or living off savings? We advise you on the exact financial and character evidence property managers require to approve non-standard applicants.

Tenancy Disputes, Rent Hikes & Lease Negotiations

Unbiased guidance on navigating excessive rent increase notices, negotiating lease transfers, ending fixed terms early without penalty, requesting urgent repairs, and preparing for state tenancy tribunals (VCAT, QCAT, ACAT, SACAT).
