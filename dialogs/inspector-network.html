About Our Inspector Team

Our inspector team brings a wide range of customer service and professional backgrounds, united by a genuine interest in property and helping people find the right place to call home.

Inspectors attend open-for-inspections and private appointments on behalf of our clients to evaluate potential properties for overall condition and suitability. Our clients receive honest, frank, and unbiased assessments covering both the highlights and any red flags.

What Our Inspectors Do

Physical Attendance On-Site

Representing clients at scheduled viewings across Melbourne, Brisbane, Canberra, and Adelaide, arriving promptly to liaise with the leasing agent.

Live Video Streaming & 4K Walkthroughs

Walking clients through properties via Zoom, FaceTime, or WhatsApp in real time, or capturing clear, steady high-definition video walkthroughs with live commentary.

Condition & Defect Examination

Testing tap pressure, shower drainage, window locks, and power outlets; checking for damp or cupboard odours; evaluating storage, acoustic levels, and natural light orientation.

Neighbourhood & Street Evaluation

Observing surrounding street noise, parking permit availability, building maintenance, and the overall feel of the local pocket.
