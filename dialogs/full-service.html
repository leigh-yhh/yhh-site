The 6-Stage Relocation Process

1 Consultation

We work through your wish list, including any specific requirements (e.g. location, schools, transport, security, etc.)

2 Research

We conduct a comprehensive analysis of ON and OFF market properties to identify those that best suit your requirements.

3 Inspection

We provide a curated shortlist of properties and arrange to inspect your preferred options with you, or on your behalf, with a live video walk through.

4 Application

We prepare your application, submit it on your behalf, via the agents preferred on-line portal and work with the Property Manager to get you approved.

5 Review

We review the lease agreement and can negotiate certain terms on your behalf (e.g. start date).

6 Acceptance

We are available to answer questions that may arise and offer advice before you move in and throughout your tenancy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this service for?

People who have specific requirements, are unable to conduct their own search (e.g. relocating from interstate or overseas), or do not "fit the box" (e.g. have no recent rental history).

How much does this service cost?

Because every property search is unique, if you are considering our Full Service Package, we recommend contacting us for a confidential, obligation free discussion, of your situation and our fees.

How long does this service take?

This depends on a number of factors, like your requirements and the state of the rental market. However, as a guide, we recommend clients engage us at least 4 weeks prior to their required lease start date.
